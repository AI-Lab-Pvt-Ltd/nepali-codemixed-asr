# Code of Conduct

We expect respectful, constructive participation. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code with evidence, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or exclude participants in response to violations. Enforcement should be proportionate, protect reporters' privacy, and allow affected participants to provide context.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an established AI Lab Pvt. Ltd. contact. Maintainers must publish a monitored private reporting channel before launch and handle reports involving themselves through another company representative.
